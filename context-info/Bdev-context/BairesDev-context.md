# BairesDevServiceDescription [ID:11]

BairesDev's services: nearshore software outsourcing and staff augmentation services with the best 1% of Software Engineers in Latin America. The software engineers are in the same time zone, speak English fluently, have a great attitude, and advanced technical skills.

Our unique selling proposition includes:
1) Senior-Only Talent: Our engineers average over 10 years of hands-on experience. 
2) Same Time Zone Collaboration: our engineers work in the same time zone, making real-time communication more effective.
3) Quick Onboarding: Integrate the top 1% of Latin American tech talent within less than 2 weeks. Each of our software engineers is meticulously screened through a multi-step vetting process.
4) We have much better quality than what companies can hire locally while also having a lower cost.


# Staff Augmentation (Extended Teams) Service Description [ID:5484]

We offer our base of software engineers to the client so they may interact as if they were the client’s own employees, with minimum management taking part only to guarantee the best performance of our employees. This service represents about 80% of the company’s revenue.

Main benefits for the Client
a) Increase velocity
b) Access to Top Talent
c) Flexibility 
d) Scale quicker
e) Improve time to market

Main benefits for BairesDev
a) Low risk
b) Foster headcount growth
c) Easier engagements to manage

This model’s main validation is the role's technical requirements, engagement period, and budget. Clients that need part-time developers, short-period engagements, or have a low budget may not be fit for BairesDev, and after trying all possible solutions to retain them, we may also send them to our Partner Company.


# Delivery Teams Service Description [ID:N/A]

At Delivery Team projects, we assume responsibility for the delivery of the final product, but in some cases, the scope may not be fully defined due to different reasons (uncertainty, business needs, evolving scenarios, etc) in order to successfully manage these challenges BairesDev relies on Scrum, a framework that allows us to focus on delivering products with the highest business value in the shortest time.

Delivery Team projects stand at a middle ground, in terms of scope definition, between Staff Augmentation and Fixed Price projects.
